Same Goat

We're all in the same goat.

Same Goat is a chore chart for families. Kids finish chores and earn points, parents confirm, and everyone pitches in. It works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Private by design. Your family's chores, points, photos and moods live in your own iCloud account. There are no ads, no tracking, no AI services and no accounts to create.
