Same Goat

Same Goat privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Same Goat is a chore chart for families. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: it stays with your family.

What the app stores

  • Names and emoji avatars for the people in your household
  • Chores, notes, due dates, and photos kids attach when they finish a chore
  • Points, rewards, reward requests, and pocket-money payouts if you turn on allowance mode
  • Projects, family mood check-ins (a 1–5 face each day) and any note added to one
  • Your household's settings, such as the parent code and which features you use

Where it's stored

Everything is stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. A household you create lives in your private iCloud database; people you invite see it through iCloud sharing. The developer of Same Goat has no access to this data and runs no servers of its own.

A few settings stay only on each device, such as whether it's a shared family device and which notifications it has already shown. The home-screen widget reads a summary of today's chores that the app saves on the same device.

Family mood

If your family uses mood check-ins, each person's daily face is visible to the parents in the household; kids see the family average and their own. "What we've noticed" and the mood booster ideas are worked out on your own devices from your own check-ins and chores. They are never sent to Same Goat's developer, to an AI service, or to anyone else.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No data collection by the developer
  • No AI or other outside service sees your family's data

Children

Same Goat is meant to be set up and managed by parents. Kids' information is only visible to the members of their own household. A parent can remove a kid's profile, and everything belonging to it, under Family at any time.

Deleting your data

Remove people under Family, or delete the app and its data in iCloud through Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Same Goat. Leaving a shared household through the iCloud sharing options removes your access to it.
